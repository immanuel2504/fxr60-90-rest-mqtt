The spec's example is:
    {"country":"Canada","standardname":"CANADA_FCC_15"}

Neither value exists on this reader. Its supported region is the single combined
entry "United States/Canada", and its standards are US_FCC_15 / _A / _B / _C /
_CUSTOM. There is no bare "Canada" and no "CANADA_FCC_15".

This is most likely a SKU difference rather than a defect - a Canada-specific
unit may well report those values. But the documented example does not work on a
US/Canada reader, and the spec gives no indication that its example is
region-specific. Worth either using a universally valid example or noting that
valid values are per-device and must come from supportedRegionList /
supportedStandardList first.
