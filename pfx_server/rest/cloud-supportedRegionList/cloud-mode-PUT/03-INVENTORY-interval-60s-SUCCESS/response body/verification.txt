TAG RESULT over 20s:
  unique tags  30
  total reads  30
  reference tags:
    e2806894000040017790e471  reads=1  rssi=-38..-38  ant=[1]
    e2806894000040017790ac71  reads=1  rssi=-45..-45  ant=[1]

*** unique == total reads. Every tag reported EXACTLY ONCE. ***

That is the whole point of INVENTORY mode: modeSpecificSettings.interval sets how
often each tag may be re-reported, and at 60 s no tag can appear twice inside a
20s window. Both reference tags read exactly once.

Compare 04-INVENTORY-interval-5s-SUCCESS/, which is the identical config with
interval dropped to 5 s and produces multiple reads per tag - that A/B pair is
what proves the field is honoured rather than merely stored.
