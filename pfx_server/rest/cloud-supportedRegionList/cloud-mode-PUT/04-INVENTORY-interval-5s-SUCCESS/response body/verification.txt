*** THE A/B COMPARISON ***

                          interval 60 s      interval 5 s
  window                  20 s               22 s
  unique tags             30                 30
  total reads             30                 101
  ref tag ...e471 reads   1                  4
  ref tag ...ac71 reads   1                  5

Same tags, same power, same antenna - only the interval changed. The reference
tags went from 1 read to 4 and 5, and 22 s / 5 s = 4.4 expected reports per tag.
Observed 4 and 5.

That arithmetic is the point: the reporting rate matches the configured interval,
so INVENTORY mode's deduplication is real and predictable, not approximate.

USEFUL FOR INTEGRATORS: interval is the knob that controls event volume. At 60 s
a tag sitting in the field generates 1 event per minute; at 5 s it generates 12.
For a busy portal that is the difference between a manageable event stream and
flooding the data endpoint.
